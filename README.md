# Gyudon Kitchen

A small, offline-friendly cooking game about making a Japanese beef bowl. Work through ingredient gathering, rice preparation, warishita sauce, onion slicing, beef simmering, and final plating.

![Screenshot placeholder: Gyudon Kitchen gameplay](assets/screenshot-placeholder.svg)

## How to play

Open the page, choose a difficulty, and follow the instructions in each kitchen stage. Collect the recipe ingredients, rinse and measure rice, balance the sauce, slice and soften the onion, simmer the beef, then assemble and serve your bowl. Each stage has a hint and a retry option. Controls work with mouse, touch, and keyboard.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No install, build step, network connection, or server is required.

## Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. Push this project to the `main` branch of a GitHub repository.
2. Open **Settings > Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
4. Open the published URL shown in the Pages settings.

## The recipe

This game is based on a simple two-serving gyudon:

- 300 g thinly sliced beef (ribeye or chuck)
- 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cups cooked Japanese short-grain rice (about 1 cup uncooked)
- 120 ml dashi
- 2 tbsp soy sauce (shoyu)
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp sake
- 1 tsp sugar
- Optional: beni shoga (pickled red ginger), sliced green onion, shichimi togarashi, or a raw/onsen egg

Rinse and cook the rice. Bring dashi, sake, mirin, sugar, and soy sauce to a gentle simmer. Cook the onion until soft, add the beef in small portions, skim the aku (foam), and simmer just until no longer pink. Serve over rice with a little sauce and toppings.
