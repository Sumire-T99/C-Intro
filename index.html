A NOTE FROM THE KITCHEN

How to play

Follow the little prompts from counter to table. Tap, click, or use Tab and Enter to interact. On the onion board, use the arrow keys to position your knife and Space to slice.

Choose a pace that feels right: Easy gives more forgiving timing and visible hints, Normal is balanced, and Chef keeps the hints tucked away.

Every stage can be retried without losing the rest of your bowl. There is no rush except the gentle simmer timer.
